Upcoming work on deformed wing virus and its spread through bee populations, in the Wilfert group.

I completed my doctorate in Evolutionary Biology at Freie Universität Berlin in 2026, working with Dr Charlotte Rafaluk-Mohr and Prof. Jens Rolff on virulence evolution in entomopathogenic fungi across insect and nematode host systems.

I will be starting as a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Regensburg, joining the Wilfert group to study virus evolution in bees. My work combines experimental evolution, infection assays, and genomic analysis to ask why pathogens become more or less dangerous to their hosts.
